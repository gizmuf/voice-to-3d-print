# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Pulsai 3D community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and prioritizing the
community's well-being. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or
attention, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, and
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
issues, and other participation that is inconsistent with this Code of
Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential report, use the repository's private reporting channel:

https://github.com/gizmuf/voice-to-3d-print/security/advisories/new

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters. Enforcement may
range from a private correction or warning through a temporary or permanent
ban, proportionate to impact and repeated behavior.
